Power system for a high-altitude balloon

As part of the power engineering sub-division of a multi-disciplinary student team, I helped develop a flight-ready product. I designed LTspice schematics, including a buck converter, which steps a battery’s voltage down for the electronics.

I also researched battery configurations for low-pressure, low-temperature environments, weighing safety, cost and efficiency, and developed battery safety testing experiments that apply physics principles to the risks of operating in extreme conditions.

Physics lab (PHS3000)

Muon lifetime experiment

Muons are short-lived particles that rain down from cosmic rays. By detecting when they decay and fitting the decay times, you can measure their lifetime and, from that, estimate the Fermi constant, a number that sets the strength of the weak nuclear force.

I wrote Python scripts to analyse and fit the data, and wrote up the results as a lab report in LaTeX.

  • Python
  • Data fitting
  • LaTeX
  • Particle physics

Alpha-particle range experiment

Measuring the range of alpha particles emitted from an americium-241 source. This was achieved by varying the air pressure of the chamber through which they travelled, measuring their final energy and number with a photodiode, and exploring approximation techniques to translate this into range measurements.

To estimate the number of particles that reached the detector, I developed a skewed-Gaussian model and a corresponding integration method to account for the naturally left-skewed data.

  • Data fitting
  • Nuclear physics
