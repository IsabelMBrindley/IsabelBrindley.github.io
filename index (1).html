Thesis

Understanding the growth and electronic properties of rare earth Kagome metals

Physics research project (PHS3350), Monash University, supervised by Dr. Mark Edmonds, November 2025.

Kagome metals have atoms arranged in a woven pattern of triangles and hexagons, which makes their electrons behave in unusual ways. This could help build faster, lower-energy computers. I grew ultra-thin crystal films of one of these metals, TbMn6Sn6, then studied them at the Australian Synchrotron using X-ray and angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy. The measurements showed early signs of the two features the theory predicts, and an unexpected chemical signal that needs more work to explain.
